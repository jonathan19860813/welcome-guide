# How to unlock the Welcome Guide Builder with your email

No key to copy and paste. You only need the email address you use for your purchase.

**Open:** https://jonathan19860813.github.io/welcome-guide/unlock.html

1. Type your email address and tap **Email me a code**.
   - **Bought on Etsy? The first time,** also tap *"Add your order number"* and type your Etsy order number.
     You'll find it in the Etsy app or website under **Purchases**, or in your Etsy order confirmation email.
2. Check your inbox for an email with a **6-digit code**. It comes from **Welcome Guide**. If you can't see it within a couple of minutes, check the **Updates** or **Promotions** tab in Gmail, and your spam folder.
3. Type the code on the page. The builder opens, unlocked and ready to use.

**Good to know**
- The code works for 15 minutes and only once. If it expires, tap **Send a new code**.
- New phone or computer? Open the same page and use the same email. You'll get a new code and the same unlock.
- Just placed your Etsy order? It can take up to 30 minutes for your order to be recognised. If you entered your
  order number, we'll email you as soon as it's confirmed.
- Your guests never need a code. The guest link and QR code you share always work.
- Each purchase unlocks one email address. Need to change it? Message us through Etsy with your order number.
